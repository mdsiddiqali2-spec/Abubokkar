About

Who Am I?

Hi! I'm Abu Bakkar Siddiq. I am a data-focused professional with expertise in Data Analysis, Microsoft Excel, Microsoft PowerPoint, and professional digital productivity. I also work as a trainer in Microsoft productivity tools, helping learners turn complex information into clear, practical, and useful outputs.

Alongside data and Microsoft tools, I have a strong professional interest in Artificial Intelligence. I research AI applications, prompt engineering, and practical ways to use AI tools effectively for research, productivity, analysis, content development, and problem-solving.

My professional focus is built around three connected areas: turning data into meaningful insights, teaching people to use Microsoft tools effectively, and exploring how AI and professional prompting can improve modern knowledge work. I believe technology becomes truly valuable when it is practical, understandable, and applied to real-world needs.
